### What did I, Claude Code, or my agents learn from this

- **Paired analyzers earn their cost on test-gap findings** — gatherers list gaps from the diff alone; the analyzer, reading the full test file, dropped four false ones. Keep the analyzer step for split reviews.
- **A test that pins a value can protect a bug** — `route.test.ts` asserted `mif1` → AVIF. When a finding contradicts a test, check what the test was guarding before trusting it.
- **Widening a sniff needs the caller to read more bytes** — the helper fix alone would have passed its unit tests while the route still sliced 12 bytes; only the route-level upload test proves the two agree.
