- **Dummy env vars run the env-gated suites in a worktree** — `DATABASE_URL=postgresql://test:test@localhost:5432/test NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL=https://roland.leth.ro`; every such test mocks Prisma. All 208 files passed that way.
