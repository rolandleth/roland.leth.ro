[source](dev-journal/2026-10-11.md), [source](dev-journal/2026-10-19.md), [source](dev-journal/2026-10-20.md)
- **Scheduled content moved from Vercel Cron Jobs to Vercel Schedules** — 2026-11-10 replaced `crons` with `schedules` in `vercel.json`: `revalidate-scheduled` hourly (Cron Jobs on Hobby capped it at once a day, so posts waited up to ~25h), `ping` still daily, both with `requestMethod: "GET"` because path schedules default to POST. `WINDOW_HOURS` went 50 → 4 by the unchanged rule (2 × interval for a missed or late run, plus 15-min jitter, plus 1h DST, rounded up); `windowInvariant.test.ts` now parses `schedules` and also guards GET and the absence of `crons`. Rejected every 15 minutes (too much of the beta 5,000-run Hobby cap) and private function targets (`schedule/v1beta`, new dependency). Comments outside the route went cadence-free ("scheduled-content run"). [source](dev-journal/2026-11-10.md)
