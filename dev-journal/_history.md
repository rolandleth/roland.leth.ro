- **Auth rate-limit hardening** — `IP_HASH_SECRET` HMAC keying 06-10: `clientBucketKey` HMACs leftmost XFF IP (GDPR: plain SHA-256 is reversible at IPv4 scale ~4B addresses); global-bucket fallback when secret absent (some limiting beats none). Hardened 06-13: keyspace partitioned (`ip:<hash>` vs literal `"global"`, formally disjoint under `rl:login` prefix); bucket key hoisted to outer scope (HMAC runs once per request); log levels split (routine 429 → `info`, Redis-down fail-open → `error`); five new tests. Sources: [dev-journal/2026-06-10.md](dev-journal/2026-06-10.md), [dev-journal/2026-06-13.md](dev-journal/2026-06-13.md).

Multi-round "corner halo" bug root-caused not to the backdrop but to never rounding the *visible* image — `object-contain` letterboxes and container `rounded-xl` misses the image; fixed by aspect-true `next/image` sizing (`width={0} height={0}` + `h-auto w-auto max-h-* max-w-* rounded-xl`).
