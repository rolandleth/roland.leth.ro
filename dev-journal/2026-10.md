## Learnings

- **Measure copy in the real font** — the writer agent's per-character width estimates missed by a line at 1,040px. [source](dev-journal/2026-10-02.md)
- **The browser pane misreports motion and hit targets** — frozen transitions and smooth scroll, stale post-click screenshots, scaled emulation drawn into a corner. [source](dev-journal/2026-10-02.md)
- **Target a database by env, not by file** — `dotenv/config` doesn't override a set variable, so `env DATABASE_URL=…` points Prisma commands at dev without touching `.env`. [source](dev-journal/2026-10-03.md)
- **Staging around another session's uncommitted work** — filtered patches with `git apply --cached`, or HEAD plus only these changes put in the index with `hash-object` and `update-index`. [source](dev-journal/2026-10-03.md)
