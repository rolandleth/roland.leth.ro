## Learnings

- **Path schedules default to POST** — a schedule without `requestMethod: "GET"` gets a 405 from a GET-only route handler on every run. [source](dev-journal/2026-11-10.md)
- **The checked-in Prisma client embeds the schema text** — `src/generated/prisma/internal/class.ts` carries `schema.prisma` verbatim and the build doesn't regenerate it, so comment-only edits drift but break nothing. [source](dev-journal/2026-11-10.md)
- **Separate "breaks" from "drifts" before calling something required** — two overstatements this session: the UTC offset as a new "wrong time", and "needs regenerating" for comment-only schema edits. [source](dev-journal/2026-11-10.md)
