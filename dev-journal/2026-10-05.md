- **Blob data transfer on Hobby** — 10 GB a month, shared by images and video; past it Blob stops serving for up to 30 days, images included. A widely shared video post is the trigger. The renderer keys on the URL's extension only, so videos can move to another host (Cloudflare R2 has no transfer charge); `media-src` would need that host.

- **Old posts with images wrapped in raw HTML** — `<div class=centered>![…](…)</div>` and `<img class=…>` blocks are dropped whole, since the pipelines drop raw HTML. Not checked on the live pages; revisit when those posts are next touched.
- **Media of rows deleted before this change** — nothing swept `posts/…` or `projects/…` blobs on an admin delete until now, so earlier project deletes may have left blobs. `blob:prune-uploads` only looks at UUID keys.
