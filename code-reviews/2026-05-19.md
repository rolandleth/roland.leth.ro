## Architecture & Coupling

- **[src/lib/auth/env.ts — bucket placement]** — `env.ts` is consumed by 5 sites; only 2 are auth (`auth/auth.ts`, `app/api/auth/login/route.ts`). The other 3 are non-auth — `db/db.ts:3` pulls `getDatabaseUrl`, `api/keepalive.ts:2` pulls `getRedisConfig`, `app/api/cron/ping/route.ts:4` pulls `getCronSecret`. Locating env under `auth/` forces `db/` and `api/` to import "up" into `auth/` for non-auth reasons, inverting the natural dependency direction (`auth/` should be the leaf, not a hub). The journal's "most consumers are auth-adjacent" framing doesn't match the call graph. Lower-friction placements: hoist env to `src/lib/env.ts` (single-file exception to bucketing), or rename the bucket `config/` to reflect what's actually inside. Not flagged in 2026-05-18's bucket-boundary watch-out — adding it here.
