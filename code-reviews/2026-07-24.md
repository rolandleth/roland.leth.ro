## Architecture & Coupling

- [x] **[src/app/api/feed/[section]/route.ts + src/proxy.ts — dual feed URLs]** — The same feed is now reachable with 200 at two crawlable paths: `/api/feed/:section` (pinned as the Atom `<id>`, re-allowed in robots) and `/blog/:section/feed.xml` (the advertised `rel="self"`/autodiscovery URL). There is no HTTP-level canonicalization between them, and the redirect funnel (`FEED_REGEX`, plus the uncommitted `FEED_ALIAS_REGEX`) 301s `/feed`, `/:section/feed`, `/rss`, `/feed.xml` to `/api/feed/:section` — i.e. *not* the pretty URL the design advertises as canonical. Low-risk for feed readers (they de-dup on `<id>`/`rel=self`), but the "canonical is the pretty URL" intent and the funnel/robots posture point in different directions; confirm the divergence is deliberate. **Resolved 2026-07-24**: confirmed deliberate — `<id>` stays `/api/feed/:section` as the permanent subscriber-dedup key; `rel="self"`/autodiscovery use the pretty URL so readers migrate over time; the funnel targets `/api/feed` for uniformity with the long-lived `/feed` 301, avoiding churn on a cached redirect. Rationale recorded in `dev-journal/2026-07-24.md`.
