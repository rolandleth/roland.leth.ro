## Code Quality

- ~~**[src/app/globals.css — `.link, .prose a` combined rule]**~~ — **Withdrawn 2026-05-10.** On closer reading, `font-weight: inherit` on standalone `.link` is a no-op: `<a>` is inline and inherits font-weight from its parent by default. The reason `.prose a` needs explicit `inherit` is to override Tailwind typography's `font-weight: 600` on `.prose a`; there's no such override on `.link` to fight against. A `.link` inside a `<strong>` would inherit bold both before and after the merger. The merge is a clean refactor with no blast radius.
