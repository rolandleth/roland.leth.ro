That is 470 deleted lines with no coverage lost.

The risk sits in two places, both of the same shape: a fix that closed the named instance and left the class, then documented the class as closed. The session-check commit guarded the two pages the last review named while the four edit pages — heavier reads, rendering record data rather than a project list — keep a `generateMetadata` guard that logs and falls through, and the dashboard, which every protected page links into, got nothing. The helper's own docblock now tells the next author those pages are covered, and the contract test's prose agrees, so the suite will stay green over it. Second, `BlogPostList`'s empty-page guard was deleted on the strength of a "agree by construction" claim that the two cache entries do not support, and the same change split a clock read that previously kept rows and totals on one instant. Neither the claim nor the state it protects against has a test. Nothing here is publicly exposed and nothing regressed a live surface — the middleware still gates every admin path — but two guards were removed against reasoning that is more confident than the code.
