## Risk Assessment

**Medium.** Sixteen commits reshaped load-bearing plumbing: legacy routing moved off billed compute into `next.config.ts`, blog pagination moved from `?page=` to path segments and went static, the hourly `revalidate = 3600` backstop was replaced by a single daily cron, the middleware matcher narrowed to two explicit namespaces, and the `/og` route became a build script. Contracts mostly narrowed rather than widened, which is the right direction, and the work is unusually well documented. Type-check is clean and 2048 of 2049 tests pass.

Two things hold the rating at Medium rather than Low. First, the cron is now the sole mechanism surfacing scheduled content, and its correctness rests on a `WINDOW_HOURS ≥ 2 × interval + 1` relation that lives in prose across two files — a schedule edit landed *during this review* with nothing to check it, and the one test guarding the window accepts any value from 26 hours upward. Second, `61929b8` found and fixed a genuine production defect in this same mechanism (detail pages and `.md` routes pinning a 404 rendered while a post was still future-dated, invisible because the aggregates listed it correctly); that class of prerendered-404 staleness is not yet pinned by a test, and the paginated blog route carries the same shape.

The admin guard work is a real improvement in enforceability, but `adminPageContract.test.ts` asserts source text where its API-side sibling asserts behaviour, so it delivers less than its own docblock claims. No finding here is an open security hole — the `/admin/:path*` matcher remains the primary gate and covers every case.
