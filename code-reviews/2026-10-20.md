## Risk Assessment

**Low.** Most of this batch is the store-row refactor (`f69a2525`). It folds four copies of the store-button/status row into `StoreLinks`, renames `product-hero-fade` to `product-hero-rise`, and moves the header height to `--site-header-height`. Both have tests in `ProductPage.test.tsx`, and the new `StoreLinks.test.tsx` and `ProjectLinkCta.test.tsx` cover the rows on their own. A search for the old names (`storeButton`, `product-hero-fade`, `top-14`, `-mt-14`, `StoreLink` from `ProductHero`) finds no stale callers. Every token value equals the old literal, so nothing should look different. The rest is legal-page date strings, a Prettier re-wrap of the Weekly Gym privacy page, and journal files. Nothing touches data, auth, routing or caching. The main risk is drift: the date format and the store filter are each kept in step by hand.
