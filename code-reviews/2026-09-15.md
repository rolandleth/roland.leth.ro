## Risk Assessment

**Medium.** Commit `a3af1b4` renames a stored column (`Post.summary` to `description`) and makes it an authored field that frontmatter fills. The deploy needs a manual `ALTER TABLE` first. The main risk is the PUT route: normal admin actions (the Published toggle, a body edit) replace authored descriptions and leave no trace, and this lands just before the planned `--overwrite` import of frontmatter descriptions. The 160-character cap applies on some write paths and not on others, so the edit form can reject posts that another path created. The guide `.md` route, the breadcrumb JSON-LD and `sameAs` follow the blog patterns and have tests; their gaps are newline escaping and copy. The CSS link change is small, but it makes an older unstyled-link problem on the legal pages more visible. The project-link commits (`b54d939` to `3ed20b4`) are low risk on their own: the CTA rules are now one pure function with wide unit and render tests, and the flag check closes the import reset. What is left there is stale docs and comments, one call site with no test, and test-fixture debt.
