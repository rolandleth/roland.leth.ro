## Risk Assessment

**Medium.** The range fixes a live production incident — three routes serving the 404 page under HTTP 200 — and does it the right way: route groups scoping the two list skeletons, deletion of the three detail ones, and a filesystem guard standing in for a regression test no unit suite can write. The cron rewrite is the strongest work here; `Promise.allSettled` over `all`, the `DUE_ROW_CAP` switch that falls back to a blanket bust rather than dropping rows, the single clock read, and per-half failure attribution are all correct and unusually well documented. Tests pass (2218/2218) and types are clean.

What carries the risk is narrowing. Three contracts tightened in this range — `MAX_PAGE`, `blogPagePath`'s parameter, and the section-pinned redirect sources — and two were checked against every caller while `MAX_PAGE` was not. Dropping a shared constant by a factor of 333 while reading only one of its two call sites is the single defect here that can bite without anyone noticing, because the caller it missed clamps silently and the build guard protecting the other one does not extend to it. Beyond that the concerns are guard-shaped rather than behavioural: the archive route is one deleted line from reinstating the incident with a green suite, the boundary walk stops short of `guides` and `admin`, and the lint gate has been red long enough that nobody is reading it. Nothing in the caching or revalidation plumbing looks wrong; the schema change is inert until it is pushed.
